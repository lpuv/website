## What it is

A Tetris clone that is actively against you. The repository description calls it "really stupid," which is accurate and also the design brief.

The goal is inverted: you stack pieces to reach the top of the well, not to clear lines. Every ten seconds the game rolls a chaos event. Sometimes the pieces stop colliding, or gravity flips, or everything gets bouncy, or a piece is replaced with a giant one, or the game rickrolls you. You lose if fifty pieces fall past the line.

## Why it's on here

Two reasons, and I'd rather state them than pretend this is a serious project.

**It's a real engine.** Godot and GDScript were new to me. Shipping something small and complete teaches you more about a toolchain than a half-built ambitious thing does, and "complete" is a skill I wanted to practise.

**It's evidence I build for the fun of it.** A portfolio where every project is carefully chosen looks like a portfolio built for the portfolio. This one exists because I thought it would be funny, and then I finished it, which is the harder half.
